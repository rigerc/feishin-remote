title: Using the app
description: Now Playing, the library, playlists, radio, the queue, and Android media controls.
---

The navigation bar has five tabs: **Playing**, **Library**, **Playlists**, **Radio**, and **Queue**. A mini player sits above it on every tab except Playing.

## Now Playing

Shows the artwork, title, artist, and album of the track playing in Feishin, with play/pause, previous, next, seek, volume, shuffle, and repeat. You can:

- Favorite the track and set a rating.
- Tap the artwork to see it fullscreen.
- Mute, then unmute to restore the previous volume.
- Expand the track details for year, genre, format, bitrate, sample rate, bit depth, track and disc number, and play count.

## Library

Search and browse **Tracks** and **Albums**. Each item's menu offers **Play**, **Play shuffled**, and, for tracks, **Track Radio** and **Add to playlist**. When you play something, choose whether it plays **Now**, **Next**, or **Last** in the queue.

## Playlists and Radio

Open a playlist to play it or pick tracks from it. The Radio tab lists the internet radio stations configured in Feishin.

## Queue

The Queue tab mirrors the live queue on your computer. Search it, tap a track to play it, **Jump to current**, drag to reorder, swipe a track away to remove it, or **Clear queue**.

Browsing, playlists, radio, and the queue need Feishin 1.16.0 or newer.

## Android media controls

When Feishin reports a current track, the app shows a media notification. The notification, lock screen, headset buttons, and Bluetooth controls all control Feishin on your computer; nothing plays on the phone.

## Themes

Switch between the light and dark theme with the sun or moon button in the top bar. With Feishin 1.16.0 or newer, turn on **Match desktop accent** on the Playing tab to use Feishin's accent colour in either theme.
